# Discovery questions

Ask one block at a time and follow the answers. These are prompts, not a form to be
completed in order.

## Opening — before any category

- What made you decide to document this now?
- If you left tomorrow, what would your replacement most need to know?
- What breaks most often?
- What keeps you up at night?
- Is there anything you know is wrong but have not been able to fix?

The last two questions usually produce the most useful material in the whole exercise.

## Business applications

- Walk me through an order arriving and being fulfilled. Which systems does it touch?
- Same for an invoice going out, and for a new employee starting.
- Which system would stop the business if it went down for a day?
- Is there a spreadsheet or small database that a process actually depends on?
- Anything still running that everyone agrees should have been replaced?

## Collaboration & productivity

- Where does email live, and who administers it?
- Where do documents actually live, versus where they are supposed to live?
- Are there personal accounts or personal storage being used for work?

## Identity & access

- Is there one directory, or several?
- What happens, step by step, when someone joins? And when someone leaves?
- Which systems have their own separate logins?
- Where is MFA enforced, and where is it not?
- Who has administrative rights, and do they use a separate account for it?

## Compute & storage

- What runs on your own hardware, and where is it physically?
- Which cloud tenants and subscriptions exist, and who pays for them?
- How old is the oldest server still doing something important?

## Network & connectivity

- How many sites, and how are they connected?
- Who provides connectivity, and what happens when a line goes down?
- What is reachable from the internet?
- Who manages the firewalls — you, or a supplier?
- When do your certificates and domains renew?

## Endpoints

- How are workstations built and kept updated?
- Are devices managed, and by what?
- Any personal devices with access to company data?

## Data protection

- What is backed up, how often, and to where?
- Is there a copy that ransomware could not reach?
- **When was the last time you restored something and it worked?**
- How long would a full recovery take, and has anyone timed it?

## Security controls

- What is on the endpoints?
- What filters email?
- Where do logs go, and does anyone look at them?
- How do you find out a system needs patching?
- Has anything happened in the last two years that you would call an incident?

## Operations

- What tells you something is broken — a monitoring system, or a phone call?
- Where do requests and incidents get recorded?
- Who is on call outside working hours?
- How does a change get made and approved?

## Integrations

- What moves data between systems automatically?
- Who built those interfaces, and who maintains them now?
- What happens when one fails — does anyone find out?

## Suppliers

- Which suppliers could stop you working if they disappeared?
- What support level do you have, and what response time?
- When do the main contracts renew?
- Who do you call at 21:00 on a Friday?

## Closing

- What did I not ask about that I should have?
- Who else should I be talking to?
